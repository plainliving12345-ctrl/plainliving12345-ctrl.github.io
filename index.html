Channel Helper

Channel Helper is a small private tool that one independent creator uses to manage the comments on their own YouTube channels.

It reads the public comments on the creator's own channels, helps the creator understand comments written in other languages, drafts short replies, and posts replies as the creator's own channel through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.
